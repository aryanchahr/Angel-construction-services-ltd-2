How We Work

Our 4-Step Design & Build Journey

A proven, structured framework that ensures your project progresses smoothly on schedule and on budget.

01

Free Site Consultation

We meet at your property to assess spatial feasibility, discuss your design vision, budget expectations, and answer technical questions.

02

Design & Planning

Detailed architectural CAD drawings, structural calculations, and seamless council planning permission and party wall submissions.

03

Fixed Contract & Build

Fixed itemized contract and dedicated project manager. Our master tradesmen execute the build with weekly milestones and tidy sites.

04

Certification & Handover

Final snagging inspection, full Building Control sign-off certificate, deep clean, and issuance of your 10-year structural warranty pack.
